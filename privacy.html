Privacy Policy

Easy Mortgage · Effective November 11, 2026
SupportPrivacy

Summary

Easy Mortgage does not collect, store or share any personal data. All calculations happen on your iPhone.

What the app stores

The app remembers the last numbers you entered (country, home price, down payment, interest rate, amortization and payment frequency) so they are there the next time you open it. This information is saved only on your device, is never sent to us or anyone else, and is deleted when you delete the app.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-up
  • No advertising and no ad tracking
  • No analytics or third-party SDKs
  • No location, contacts, photos or other personal information
  • No data sold or shared with third parties

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at https://github.com/richardliao76/easy-mortgage/issues
